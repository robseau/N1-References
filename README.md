# N1 References — Research Bibliography and Archival PDFs

This public repository collects **references 11–26** cited in *Fundamentals of N1 Arithmetic Geometry* and related RZO/N1 research. These are archival research documents, not a new mathematical certification or an assertion that the Riemann hypothesis, RTC.Exclusion, QD2, or Paper VI is closed.

## Status labels printed inside the PDFs

**Please read this before interpreting any “UNLOCKED,” “NOT LOCKED,” “THEOREM CANDIDATE,” or “RELEASE REVIEW PENDING” designation.**

The collection preserves the **original bytes** of the supplied research PDFs. A status banner records the state of a particular document **when that document was generated**. Some results were subsequently revised, audited, approved, or formally locked in separate authoritative records in [robseau/RZO](https://github.com/robseau/RZO); in other cases, only part of the mathematical content was audited or the later release was still pending. The original PDFs were *not silently rewritten* merely to update the banners.

A later lock, where independently documented, applies to its **precisely stated theorem, version, hypotheses, and scope**. It does not retroactively make every claim in an older candidate or editorial derivative into a locked theorem.

Examples of the historical distinction:

- **#11 (General Fixed-N Hankel v0.4.1-A):** the PDF calls itself an “AUDIT-REFINED REPLACEMENT TEXT — NOT LOCKED.” Consult the final fixed-index theorem's separate lock record rather than treating this historical banner as the final status.
- **#14 (Third-Branch v0.6):** the PDF explicitly distinguishes an Aristotle proof PASS from a **post-audit editorial derivative — NOT LOCKED**. It also identifies an additional observation that was **not separately audited**. A proof audit is not a blanket lock of later additions.
- **#15 (Degree Five v0.1a):** the PDF says **ARISTOTLE PASS WITH REFINEMENT; RELEASE REVIEW PENDING; NOT LOCKED**. Later certification must be checked separately.
- **#20 (Expanded Stage A v0.3-R1):** the PDF says **NOT LOCKED** at its October 19, 2026 preparation date. This is the genuine frozen v0.3-R1 historical file. See later Stage A lock records separately; do not equate the entire historical candidate with a later scope-restricted theorem.
- **#26 (Canonical Forced-Section Geometry):** the archived theorem-sheet bytes say **UNLOCKED THEOREM CANDIDATE v0.2**. A separate subsequent Aristotle audit and GitHub lock certify the **specified CFSG v0.2 scope**, without changing that frozen historical banner. [CFSG lock record](https://github.com/robseau/RZO/commit/34ab038f23ddf1b0f51e5b4404e8b075635e71ea).

**Document authenticity, mathematical auditing, and formal lock status are three different questions.** A matching archival SHA-256 proves byte identity, not theorem correctness. For mathematically load-bearing uses, consult the actual theorem sheet together with its applicable audit, final lock status, and dependency record in the source repository.

## Archival boundaries

These documents include conditional and scope-restricted statements. The collected N1 algebraic Block–Frobenius work does **not** by itself establish an all-index spectral operator identification or universal endpoint visibility. Nothing in this bibliography claims RTC.Exclusion or QD2 closure, or a Yang–Mills mass-gap proof.

The archival source of truth for theorem locks and audits is [robseau/RZO](https://github.com/robseau/RZO). If a PDF's historical banner conflicts with a later lock receipt, **cite the versioned document and the separate lock receipt together**, and preserve the scope distinction.

## Recommended citations

Cite the **individual numbered PDF** by its title, version, and date. When a theorem's certification is relevant, also cite the independently archived audit or formal lock record. Do not use this README as a substitute for those mathematical sources.
